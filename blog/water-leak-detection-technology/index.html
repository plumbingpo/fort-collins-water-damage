Smart Water Leak Detectors in Fort Collins, CO

Home / Blog / Modern Leak Detection for Homes in Fort Collins, CO

Modern water leak detection technology offers Fort Collins, CO homeowners an invaluable layer of protection against water damage. These devices can detect leaks in their earliest stages, often before visible damage occurs, and alert you immediately, potentially saving thousands in restoration costs. Below we go through the newest leak detection choices for protecting your property.

Leak Detection Systems Compared

Leak detection can be as simple as a standalone sensor or as comprehensive as a whole-home system. A point sensor is a small battery-powered unit that sits near a sink, washing machine, water heater, or other risk area and sounds an alarm if it detects water. Smart sensors join your WiFi network, so a moisture reading sends an alert to your smartphone even if you are not home. A whole-home system watches the main water line for unusual flow and can shut the water off automatically once it detects a leak.

Where to Place Leak Detectors

To get the most from your sensors, put one beside the dishwasher, by the water heater, below each bathroom sink and the kitchen sink, at the back of the washing machine, next to any refrigerator with a water line, close to the sump pump or elsewhere in the basement, along HVAC condensate drains, and in any crawl space or utility area that has plumbing.

Leak Detection by a Professional

When leaks are suspected but not visible, professional leak detection services use advanced technology to locate hidden water intrusion. Moisture under a floor or behind a wall changes the surface temperature, and thermal imaging cameras show that difference. Water flowing in a hidden pipe makes a sound, and acoustic devices are built to hear it. For precise moisture content numbers in building materials, technicians rely on moisture meters. Pairing this equipment with experienced IICRC-certified technicians lets Fort Collins Restoration locate hidden leaks and deal with them before major damage sets in.
