What Usually Goes Wrong in Thornton Homes

Most water damage calls we take in Thornton trace back to a short list of causes. If you can spot these early, you have a good chance of stopping the damage before it spreads.

Pipes: A pipe that freezes can split, and the flooding starts once the ice thaws and pressure returns. Depending on the season, the plumbing most at risk runs through outside walls, attics and garages without heat. Corroded fittings and worn supply lines can also let go without any warning in older homes. Shut off the main valve right away, because an open line can soak several rooms quickly.

Appliances: The thin hoses feeding a washer, dishwasher or ice maker do not last forever. A water heater near the end of its life can leak from the tank bottom or fail all at once. These failures do the most harm when the appliance is on an upper floor or above finished living space.

Overflows: A clogged toilet or a sink left running can put water on the floor in minutes. The water often runs under baseboards and through the ceiling of the room below. If the overflow came from the toilet bowl or a drain, treat it as contaminated and keep children and pets away.

Roof: Rain gets in through worn flashing, damaged shingles or gutters that have stopped draining. The stain on your ceiling usually shows up well after the insulation above it is soaked. We dry the framing, insulation and drywall, and a roofer needs to fix the opening so it does not return.

Basement: Once the ground outside is soaked, water finds its way into a basement through wall cracks and the seam where floor meets wall. If the sump pump sticks or the electricity goes out mid-storm, the pit fills and spills across the slab. Carpet, wall studs and drywall wick moisture upward, which is why finished lower levels take the worst of it.

Mold: Mold can start growing within 24 to 48 hours on materials that stay wet. Many mold jobs we handle in Thornton began with an old leak that was wiped up but never dried inside the wall. A musty smell, peeling paint or dark spotting near a previous repair is worth having checked.

The First Hour After Water Damage in Thornton

The hour between finding water and the crew arriving is when a homeowner in Thornton can do the most good. Look after people first and property second. If water is near outlets, cords or the electrical panel, stay out of it, and switch off power to that area only if you can reach the breaker without stepping in water. If the water is from a sewer or toilet backup, do not touch it at all.

Then shut the water off. Use the small valve behind the toilet or under the sink, or go straight to the main. Then move what you can carry: rugs, electronics, papers, and small furniture go to a dry room. Slide plastic or aluminum foil beneath the feet of anything too heavy to move, which stops stains and swelling.

Take photos and video of every affected room before you clean up, because your insurer will want to see the damage as it was. Mop or towel up small amounts of clean water if it is safe, and leave the rest to the crew heading to Thornton.
